Ready-to-use messages, each tied to a specific trigger already defined in Partnership SOP v1.0. Keep them short and warm - these are meant to sound like a person checking in, not a system notification.

1. Welcome message (on wallet activation)

Trigger: SOP #5, immediately after fee payment and credit grant

"Welcome to the Sarthy partner network! Your account's live and you've got 999 credits to start. The easiest way to get value fast: send us your first lead, or let us know what kind of leads you're hoping to receive. We'll check in soon to help you get going."

2. Acknowledgment reminder (day 5 of the 1-week window)

Trigger: SOP #7, 2 days before the acknowledgment window closes

"Quick nudge - you've got a lead waiting on your response, and the window closes in 2 days. Take a look when you get a chance, even if it's a pass."

3. Lead declined confirmation

Trigger: SOP #7, on decline within the window

"No problem - we've noted that this one wasn't a fit. No credit deduction on your end. We'll keep sending relevant leads your way."

4. Stall check-in (admin-facing, internal)

Trigger: SOP #7, when a lead crosses the 1-week acknowledged-but-no-update line

Internal note template for the Associate: "Lead [ID] acknowledged by [Partner] on [date], no update since. Reach out directly to confirm status before flagging as disputed."

5. Dormancy warning (approaching 3 months)

Trigger: SOP #21, roughly 2 weeks before the dormancy line

"We noticed it's been a while since your last referral - just checking in before anything changes on your account. Is there anything blocking you from sending a lead, or can we help you find one worth referring?"

6. Dormant status notification

Trigger: SOP #21, on crossing 3 months inactive

"Your account's moved to inactive status after 3 months without a referral, so you're currently off the public partner listing and won't receive new leads. This isn't permanent - reach out any time and we'll get you reactivated."

7. Reactivation outreach

Trigger: dormant-partner win-back campaign, SOP #17

"It's been a bit since we've connected - wanted to check in and see how things are going on your end. A lot has changed in the partner program since you were last active, including [specific update]. Worth a quick call to catch up?"

8. Monthly engagement check-in

Trigger: SOP #15, ongoing cadence

"Monthly check-in - how's the partner program working for you lately? Any leads you're sitting on that we could help move, or types of leads you'd like to see more of?"

9. Dispute acknowledgment

Trigger: SOP #9, within 24 hours of a dispute being raised

"We've received your dispute on [lead/conversion ID] and are looking into it now - you'll hear back from us within 24 hours with next steps."

10. Annual referral fee payout notice

Trigger: SOP #20, March 31st settlement

"Your annual referral fee payout for [year] is ready - [amount] based on deals closed through your referrals this year. Details and payout timing below."

11. Existing partner transition notice

Trigger: a partner who signed under the original flat 10% referral model, before this new dual-track system existed - not a new-partner onboarding message, a genuine explanation of what's changed for them

"Since you joined, we've redesigned how the partner program works - you can now earn credits by referring leads directly to other partners too, not just from Sarthy. Nothing about your existing arrangement is changing without your agreement first - we'll walk you through exactly what's new and get your sign-off before anything about your account changes."

Note: this message assumes existing partners need a new or amended agreement before moving to the new model - see the Action Item Tracker's legal review item. Don't send this at scale until that's confirmed.
