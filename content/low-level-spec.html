It doesn't restate what the BRD or Data Dictionary already cover - it adds the things only a precise technical spec needs: exact state transitions, exact formulas, and defined operations with clear inputs, outputs, and conditions.

Lead state machine

Rewritten once the Connection Request Flow was confirmed. Two states and two operations changed meaning, not just gained a new case: Introduced now fires immediately on submission (visible to all relevant parties, faded/FYI-only) instead of after validation; Acknowledged is now reached through admin taking consent from the requested part(y/ies), not the receiving partner self-servicing an accept in the app. The old 7-day self-service window and its automatic finalization at hour 168 are retired entirely - nothing in this state machine times out on its own any more. Unresolved is no longer a reachable state.

Validation and edge-case rules

  • Duplicate detection: runs at the moment of upload, before the lead reaches admin - not a background job that runs later.
  • Consent-refusal counting: 4 consecutive refusals for the same receiving partner (not 4 total, not 4 in a rolling window) flags the partner to admin. The count resets on any acceptance.
  • No acknowledgment window: retired along with the old self-service accept/decline. A Lead can sit at Introduced or Validated indefinitely - nothing about it finalizes automatically at any hour count. It only changes state when admin actively approves or a requested party refuses consent.
  • Dispute window: 14 days from the decision being disputed, in writing. Admin response target: 24 hours from that point. Unaffected by the acknowledgment-window retirement above - this is the separate, still-active window for disputing a conversion decision (Section 9 of the Playbook), not the old lead-acceptance window.
  • At-Risk and Frozen are independent triggers - evaluate both conditions separately on every wallet-affecting event, don't treat one as implying the other.
