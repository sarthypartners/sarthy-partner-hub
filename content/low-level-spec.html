This is the technical detail layer beneath the BRD - written to be technology-neutral, so it stays useful whether this gets built on Replit, migrated elsewhere later, or handed to a different team entirely. It doesn't restate what the BRD or Data Dictionary already cover - it adds the things only a precise technical spec needs: exact state transitions, exact formulas, and defined operations with clear inputs, outputs, and conditions.

  • The Data Dictionary is the source of truth for field names, types, and meanings. This document builds on it, doesn't repeat it.

Validation and edge-case rules

  • Duplicate detection: runs at the moment of upload, before the lead reaches admin - not a background job that runs later.
  • Decline counting: 4 consecutive declines (not 4 total, not 4 in a rolling window) flags the partner to admin. The count resets on any acceptance.
  • Acknowledgment window: exactly 168 hours (7 days) from the Introduced timestamp. Finalization at hour 168 is automatic, not admin-triggered.
  • Dispute window: 14 days from the decision being disputed, in writing. Admin response target: 24 hours from that point.
  • At-Risk and Frozen are independent triggers - evaluate both conditions separately on every wallet-affecting event, don't treat one as implying the other.
