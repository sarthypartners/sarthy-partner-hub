Use this every time you talk to one of the current early app users. Keep it like a real conversation, not a form you're reading out loud. The goal is honest detail, not fast answers.

Before you call

  • If you can, check what they've actually done in the app - when they last logged in, how many leads they've sent or received.
  • Know which program they're in (India or international) and how long they've been signed up, so you're not asking them to repeat things they've already told us.

How to open the call

"We're improving how the partner app works, and you're one of the few people actually using it right now - so what you tell us matters more than most feedback we get. Nothing you say changes your standing with Sarthy. We just want the honest picture of what's working and what isn't."

Questions about how they use it

  • Tell me about the last time you opened the app. What were you trying to do?
  • What do you actually use it for today - checking leads, sending referrals, checking your balance, something else?
  • Is there anything you'd expect to be able to do in the app that you're still doing over WhatsApp, email, or a phone call instead?

Questions about what's frustrating

  • Tell me about a time something in the app confused you or didn't work how you expected.
  • Has a lead or referral ever felt unclear or unfair in how it was handled? Walk me through what happened.
  • If you've tried to withdraw your credits, how did that go?
  • Is there anything you gave up trying to do in the app because it was too much hassle?

Questions about what would bring them back

  • How often do you find yourself thinking about checking the app, even if you don't always do it?
  • What would make you want to open it every week, without needing a reminder?
  • If we could add just one thing to the app, what would actually make a difference for you?

Wrapping up

  • Is there anyone else in your network you think should be part of this?
  • Can we check in with you again in a few weeks as things change?

What to do with the notes afterward

Write up the raw notes under this partner's record in the Interim Operating System. Anything that sounds like a bug or a feature request should be logged and passed on to the Technical team - the routing destination admin sends it to, per the Phase 0 Discussion Doc.

If the same theme comes up across several calls, make sure it makes its way into the write-up of how things work today, and from there into the list of problems and fixes. Don't let a strong, repeated pattern sit unused.
