The full partner lifecycle, start to finish. Partnership SOP v1.0 #14 summarizes this; this document is where the detail actually lives.

StageWhat happensWhere friction likely shows up
1. AwarenessPartner hears about the program via LinkedIn, a mailer, the WhatsApp community, or a direct approachNo consistent inbound channel - depends entirely on who's reaching out that week
2. EvaluationSarthy reviews the company profile, holds a discovery call, reads fit against service relevance, client diversity, professional standing, and a first Giver/Taker impressionInformal, conversation-based judgment - no written checklist yet
3. Agreement & PaymentReferral agreement signed digitally, programme fee paid (₹999+GST or $49), 999 opening credits grantedNone currently identified - this step is well-defined
4. First ActivityPartner either submits their first lead or receives one - this is the make-or-break moment for habit formationThe single biggest gap today - most partners never reach this stage in practice ("Part of Platform" is empty for most)
5. Active ParticipationRegular giving and receiving, wallet moving in both directions, Sarthy team checking in per SOP #15Even the few current users show infrequent activity, not sustained habit
6. Steady StatePartner settles into a Giver, Balanced, or Taker pattern (see Segmentation Framework)No current data on how long this takes or what triggers the split
7. Risk ZoneWallet trending negative, declines mounting, or activity slowing toward the 3-month dormancy lineThis is exactly where At-Risk-status outreach (Segmentation Framework) needs to intervene, before Stage 8
8a. ReactivationA partner who went quiet re-engages, usually after direct outreachNo process exists yet - this is SOP #17's remaining open piece
8b. Formal Dormancy3 months with no shared lead - removed from public listing, blocked from receiving new leads (SOP #21)Whether this actually gets communicated clearly to the partner, or just happens silently, is untested

What the interview guide should validate

This map is built from documented rules, not direct partner testimony. The Alpha Partner Interview Guide exists specifically to check whether Stage 4 and 5 actually feel this way to a real partner, or whether the friction sits somewhere this map hasn't captured yet.
