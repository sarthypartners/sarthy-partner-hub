The targets to hit before opening the platform beyond the current small Alpha group. Anchored to the one real target Sarthy has already set (SOP #18), plus a few supporting criteria to make sure "10 active partners" means genuinely active, not just signed up.

Sarthy's confirmed target

At least 10 active partners within 2 weeks of the app being fully up and running - not the current early-testing state, once it's genuinely live.

Supporting criteria - proposed, to make "active" mean something real

  • Each of the 10 must have completed at least one full lead cycle - submitted or received a lead that reached acknowledgment, not just logged in once. Note: "full lead cycle" here specifically means reaching acknowledgment - a deliberately lighter bar than the conversion-based cycle described in the SOP (Sections 6, 7, 9). The point at this stage is proving the mechanics work end to end quickly, not requiring an actual paid deal - that's what the KPI reporting in SOP Section 19 tracks over the longer term.
  • No partner among the 10 should be sitting at the -2,997 freeze threshold - a technically "active" account that's actually stuck doesn't count
  • At least one dispute or edge case (a decline, a stall, a rejection) should have been handled through the real process, not just simulated - proof the guardrails actually work under real conditions
  • The duplicate-lead detection and acknowledgment-window mechanics (BRD #5) should be confirmed working, not just specified

What happens if the 2-week window is missed

Not currently defined by Sarthy - worth raising directly rather than assuming. Does the target simply extend, or does it trigger a review of what's not working in onboarding?

Feeds into

The Migration Wave Plan, once drafted - Wave 2 shouldn't begin until these criteria are genuinely met, not just when 2 weeks have passed on the calendar.
