The targets to hit before opening the platform beyond the current small Alpha group. Anchored to the one real target Sarthy has already set (SOP #18). A few supporting criteria make sure "10 active partners" means genuinely active - not just signed up.

Sarthy's confirmed target

At least 10 active partners within 2 weeks of the app being fully live - not the current early-testing state.

Supporting criteria - proposed, to make "active" mean something real

  • Each of the 10 must have completed at least one full lead cycle - they submitted or received a lead that reached acknowledgment, not just logged in once.
  • No partner among the 10 should be sitting at the -2,997 freeze threshold. A technically "active" account that's actually stuck doesn't count.
  • At least one dispute or edge case - a decline, a stall, a rejection - should have been handled through the real process, not just simulated. This proves the guardrails actually work under real conditions.
  • The duplicate-lead detection and acknowledgment-window mechanics (BRD #5) should be confirmed working, not just specified.

Note on "full lead cycle": here it specifically means reaching acknowledgment - a deliberately lighter bar than the conversion-based cycle described in the SOP (Sections 6, 7, 9). The point at this stage is proving the mechanics work end to end quickly, not requiring an actual paid deal. Real conversions are what the KPI reporting in SOP Section 19 tracks over the longer term.

What happens if the 2-week window is missed

Not currently defined by Sarthy - worth raising directly rather than assuming. Does the target simply extend? Or does it trigger a review of what's not working in onboarding?

Feeds into

The Migration Wave Plan, once drafted. Wave 2 shouldn't begin until these criteria are genuinely met - not just when 2 weeks have passed on the calendar.
