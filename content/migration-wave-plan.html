Before any wave starts

These are real blockers, not just a checklist - see the Action Item & Blocker Tracker for current status on each:

  • The data migration spreadsheet template is built and populated with real records
  • Legal review of existing Partner 1.0-era agreements has at least started - Wave 2 specifically can't proceed without it
  • Portal access confirmed, so the real feature inventory exists and matches what this plan assumes is buildable

Wave 0 - Pilot

Who: 2-3 of the current Alpha users who already show some real usage, not zero activity.

Why first: lowest risk - these partners are already somewhat familiar with the platform, so problems surface here before a wider group sees them. This is the seed group working toward Sarthy's real target in the Graduation Criteria.

Wave 1 - Core rollout, toward the 10-partner target

Who: existing partners on the standard 10% agreement (no special exceptions), plus new applicants who complete onboarding cleanly. This is the bulk of the push toward Sarthy's confirmed target of 10 active partners within 2 weeks of full launch.

Selection order within the wave: pre-migration, there's no real giving_pattern data to sort by - every migrated partner starts blank, same as a new signup (see the Interim Operating System). Until real activity accrues, use whatever engagement signal exists in the old tracker - a partner with some history of referring or receiving under the old system is a safer early pick than one with none at all.

New applicants: the 25+ already waiting for access fold into this same wave's cadence, going through standard onboarding (SOP Section 5) rather than a separate track.

Wave 2 - Special-agreement partners

Who: the known signed exceptions from the Current-State Assessment - a reverse 60/40 profit-sharing arrangement, a flat ₹10,000-per-trial bonus, and a tiered 15%/20% commission structure.

Why held back: the 9% facilitation fee now applies to every partner, including these - but exactly how a 9% cut interacts with a 60/40 split or a flat bonus rather than a percentage isn't worked out yet. Moving too fast here risks getting the math wrong on exactly the agreements that already have real, signed money terms.

Resolved: this wave's own conditions (legal review started, fee-split math worked out) and the Alpha to Beta Graduation Criteria's readiness gate are now tied together - Wave 2 doesn't start until both are actually satisfied, not just this section's own conditions.

Wave 3 - Reactivating long-inactive partners

Who: partners from the old system with no recent activity at all - not just "Part of Platform" blank, but genuinely dormant.

How: this isn't really a migration wave so much as a reactivation campaign - use the reactivation outreach template and the rotating weekly quota already defined in the Interim Operating System, rather than a one-time push.

Pacing

A proposed starting point, not a fixed rule: a handful of partners at a time, not a bulk import all at once. With a two-person team running this manually through the Interim Operating System, moving faster than that risks quality dropping - a bad first experience for a migrated partner is harder to undo than a slower start. Worth revisiting once Wave 0 shows how much time each migration genuinely takes.
