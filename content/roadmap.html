What has to happen before what

  • We can't finish the requirements document (Phase 4) until Sarthy answers our open questions (Phase 0). Otherwise, we'd be writing requirements around rules that might still change.
  • Moving the later groups of partners onto the platform (Phase 5) depends on Phase 2 actually being finished - not just written down, but ready to use.
  • The simple working system (Phase 3) is what lets two people run Phases 1, 2, and 5 without drowning in manual work.
  • Phase 6 is the weekly rhythm of running things. It should start from day one, even before Phase 0 wraps up.

How we'll report progress to Sarthy

We're using Sarthy's own scorecard, so our updates match how they already think about this work.

What Sarthy measuresHow much it counts
Finding and signing up partners20%

How each one is actually measured lives in Partnership SOP v1.0, Section 19. Two examples: "Talking to and supporting startups" is measured by call volume and outcomes together, not just how many calls happened. "Matching partners to startups" is measured by how many leads get matched each month, and how evenly they spread across the partner base.
