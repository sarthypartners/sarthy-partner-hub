Status definitions - precise, not casual

StatusMeans exactly this
ApplicantApplied, not yet through evaluation or fee payment
ActiveOnboarded, wallet balance above -2,997, has shared at least one lead in the last 3 months
At-RiskActive, but balance at or below -1,500 (roughly halfway to the -2,997 freeze floor), or 4+ consecutive declines flagged. Confirmed: this is purely a flag, not a restriction - an At-Risk partner keeps every privilege of an Active account (can still send and receive leads normally); the only actual effect is getting bumped to the top of the Associate's outreach priority (see the Segmentation Framework). Deliberately different from giving_pattern=Taker, which is a longer-term behavioral trend, not a threshold. The two are related (a sustained Taker pattern will often eventually cross -1,500) but don't have to fire together - a single bad stretch of received leads can trigger At-Risk on an otherwise healthy Giver, and a slow long-term Taker with a big starting buffer can stay well clear of -1,500 for a long time.
Dormant3+ months with no lead shared - removed from public listing, blocked from receiving new leads, but not frozen for balance reasons
FrozenBalance has hit -2,997 - blocked from receiving new leads until topped up to +999. Distinct from Dormant, and a partner could technically be both. If both apply: pay the top-up first, then send a lead - the top-up clears Frozen status; sending a lead is what clears Dormant.
ExitedAccount closed, by the partner's choice or Sarthy's, per the guardrails in SOP §13
