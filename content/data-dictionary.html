Status definitions - precise, not casual

StatusMeans exactly this
ApplicantApplied, not yet through evaluation or fee payment
ActiveOnboarded, wallet balance above -2,997, has shared at least one lead in the last 3 months
At-RiskActive, but balance at or below -1,500 (roughly halfway to the -2,997 freeze floor), or 4+ consecutive declines flagged. Confirmed: this is purely a flag, not a restriction - an At-Risk partner keeps every privilege of an Active account and can still send and receive leads normally. The only actual effect is getting bumped to the top of the Associate's outreach priority (see the Segmentation Framework). Deliberately different from giving_pattern=Taker, which is a longer-term behavioral trend, not a threshold. The two are related - a sustained Taker pattern will often eventually cross -1,500 - but don't have to fire together. A single bad stretch of received leads can trigger At-Risk on an otherwise healthy Giver, and a slow long-term Taker with a big starting buffer can stay well clear of -1,500 for a long time.
Dormant3+ months with no lead shared - removed from public listing, blocked from receiving new leads, but not frozen for balance reasons
FrozenBalance has hit -2,997 - blocked from receiving new leads until topped up to +999. Distinct from Dormant, and a partner could technically be both. If both apply: pay the top-up first, then send a lead. The top-up clears Frozen status. Sending a lead is what clears Dormant.
ExitedAccount closed, by the partner's choice or Sarthy's, per the guardrails in SOP #13

What this document deliberately doesn't cover yet

Gamification fields for the dormancy/activity system (SOP #21) aren't defined here since that mechanic itself isn't designed yet. A startup-side activity signal is also missing - the rule that a startup stays in the ecosystem indefinitely and can only go dormant, with no expiry, is now confirmed (see the User Journey Map, Finding 3), but no field here tracks it yet the way last_lead_shared_date and giving_pattern track a partner. This dictionary gets extended, not rewritten, whenever a new SOP section resolves - see the dependency map.
