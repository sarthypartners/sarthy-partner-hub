Why this order

The first several items (1-7) are about learning what's true today, instead of guessing. Items 8-11 turn what was learned into one clear rulebook: Partnership SOP v1.0. Each one was written directly into its own section, not as a separate document that gets summarized afterward. The last items (12-16) put that rulebook into action and measure whether it's working.
