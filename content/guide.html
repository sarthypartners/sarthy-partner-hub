Read this page first. It explains two things: how to move around this hub, and what to check whenever a change on one page affects another.

How to move around

  • The left sidebar is split into categories, like "Strategic Planning" or "Evidence & Assessment."
  • Each category has a number, showing a suggested reading order. You don't have to follow it — jump to whatever needs work right now.
  • Every document in a category is always visible. You don't need to click anything open first.
  • Hover over a category or document name. A short note pops up telling you what's inside.
  • Each document has a colored dot. Green means finished and confirmed. Amber means drafted, but still needs sign-off. Grey means it hasn't been written yet.
  • Underlined text inside a document is a real link. Click it, and it jumps straight to that document.
  • "Version history," at the bottom of the sidebar, lists every change made to the hub.

A habit to keep

This hub is just a file, not a smart system. It can't notice on its own when a change in one document should also change another.

So we follow one habit: whenever a document changes, check the table below. See what else it affects. Fix those too, in the same sitting.

How the Playbook is organized

Partnership SOP v1.0 is the main rulebook. It covers how partners join, stay engaged, get grouped, and get help when something goes wrong.

A few documents stay separate on purpose:

  • The Communication Template Library holds ready-to-use scripts, not rules.
  • The Journey Map and Segmentation Framework hold research and analysis. They feed into the SOP. They are not the SOP itself.
  • The technical documents are written for developers, not for partners or Sarthy staff.

Want to know why the Playbook is structured this way, or what it used to look like? See Design History & Decisions.

What I'll do without being asked

  • Every time we update something in the left column of the table below, I'll check the right column. I'll fix it, or tell you clearly that nothing needed to change.
  • Every bigger change gets a note in Version History.
  • If I find a connection between documents that isn't in the table yet, I'll add it.

What you and your Associate should do

  • If either of you changes a document without asking me first, tell me next time we talk.
  • If you see an amber or grey dot, it's a reminder that something else in the hub might still be waiting on it.
